TIEGCM version comparison maps

Hourly differences between two configurations: the one before the minus sign minus the reference after it. Each member is differenced first, then the 10 members are averaged. The time bar controls all figures: press play to step hour by hour, drag the slider to change the time, and choose the comparison, quantity and height.

  • Red: the configuration before the minus sign is larger; blue: smaller
  • Small dots: fewer than 8 of the 10 members have the sign of the mean
  • Hatching: at least one member has no value at this height

Height

hmF2 and NmF2 are the height and the electron density of the largest Ne in each model column, over the whole column, on the model’s own levels without interpolation, computed member by member. Where the F layer is weak the largest Ne can sit in the lower ionosphere, so hmF2 can jump by a few hundred km. The hmF2 difference is in km; the NmF2 difference is in % of the reference. They have no height, so the height buttons do not apply.

Globe
